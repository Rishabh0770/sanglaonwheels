Our Story

Sangla On Wheels began with a simple frustration: fixed-group tour packages never fit the way we wanted to see our own mountains. Years of driving these valleys — for family, for friends, for anyone who asked — convinced us the best Himalayan trips aren't packaged, they're planned around the people taking them.

With our roots planted in Sangla, Kinnaur, we've grown from a handful of local drivers to a trusted name for private road trips across Kinnaur, Spiti and the Trans-Himalaya. We've driven every stretch between Shimla and Chandratal ourselves — the shortcuts, the weather windows, the villages worth stopping in.

What sets us apart is our commitment to personalised journeys. No two travellers are alike, and no two itineraries should be either — whether you're chasing high-altitude adventure, quiet valley towns, or a bit of both.

What We Do

At Sangla On Wheels, we bring the high valleys of Kinnaur and Spiti closer to you. From the last village on the Indo-Tibet road to monasteries older than nations, we design private road trips that blend comfort, culture and altitude. Our team curates itineraries, arranges permits and stays, and handles every mountain-road detail so you can simply take in the view.

02

Where We Go

With Sangla On Wheels, every high-altitude corner of the Trans-Himalaya becomes accessible. From the apple orchards of Kinnaur to the cold desert monasteries of Spiti, the last-village calm of Chitkul, and the star-filled nights at Chandratal — we take you to places where the road itself is the destination.
